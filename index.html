The ones you never answer are the ones you never hear about.

An inquiry comes in at four in the afternoon while you are up a ladder. You see it, you mean to get to it, and then three more days of work happen. Nobody complains. They just call the next company.

You cannot chase what you cannot see, and a phone notification clears itself the moment you glance at it. A list does not.

What it does

Three things, and it does them on its own.

You point your website form notifications at a private address. From then on this happens without anybody pressing anything.

  1. 01

    It writes every inquiry down

    The moment one lands you get an alert carrying one line saying what they want, how soon they said they need it, their number, and a reply already drafted in your voice that you can send, change or ignore.

  2. 02

    It keeps reminding you

    At the end of the day you get one email listing whoever has not heard back yet, longest wait first, and it keeps asking until you have dealt with them. Robocalls and sales pitches are set aside so they never nag you and never count against you.

  3. 03

    It counts what was lost

    At the start of each month you get one report: how many inquiries came in, how many you answered, and how many never got an answer at all. If that last number is zero month after month, I will tell you so and stop billing you.

How it works

Four steps, and the first month costs nothing.

  1. One question

    Tell me where an inquiry from your website actually lands today. If the honest answer is that everything comes by phone or Facebook, I will say this is not for you rather than sell it to you anyway.

  2. Three minutes

    You add one forwarding rule in your own email so a copy of your form notifications reaches a private address I give you. I send screenshots for every step. I never ask for your password and I never see your inbox.

  3. A free month

    It runs at full strength for thirty days with no card and nothing to cancel. Alerts, the end of day list, your own page, all of it. You get to see it catch something before money is mentioned.

  4. Then you decide

    On day thirty you get the first count and I ask. Yes is $397 a quarter with no setup fee. No means no invoice, ever. Either way you keep the month.

Why it matters

What changes on Monday morning.

You stop carrying it

Who is waiting stops being something you have to hold in your head between jobs. It is written down, and it stays written down until you deal with it.

Nothing quietly disappears

A notification clears itself the second you glance at it. This does not. It keeps asking, and at the end of the month it says out loud what never got answered.

Your customers hear from you, not me

Replies are drafted, never sent. Every button opens your own phone or your own mail app. I never contact your customers, and I never will.

Nothing to learn, nothing to cancel

No app, no login, no new place to check. One forwarding rule switches it on and deleting that rule switches it off, the same minute.
