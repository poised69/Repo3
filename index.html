What Poised Automation does

Three moves, running quietly in the background.

Every system is built around the same simple shape. Watch, decide, act. All of it wired into the tools you already use.

  1. 01

    Detect

    The system watches for the signal that matters. A new lead, an order, a form, a message, a deadline creeping up. It never gets distracted and never forgets to look.

  2. 02

    Decide

    This is the engineered part. We map your real decision rules first. What counts as urgent, where each case belongs, what has to be true before anything moves. When a case is genuinely open ended, like reading a message or drafting a reply, an AI model handles that one step and hands the result back.

  3. 03

    Act, with you in the loop

    It logs, files, notifies and drafts. Anything that leaves the building or costs money waits for your approval, usually one tap away in a message. Nothing sensitive happens without a sign-off.

How it works

Four steps, start to running system.

  1. Discover

    A short conversation to find the one real bottleneck worth automating first. Not a roadmap of twelve. The single thing that costs the most and repeats the most.

  2. Design

    Map the exact workflow on paper. What triggers it, what the rules decide, which cases genuinely need judgement, what data it needs, and exactly where you check in before anything goes out.

  3. Build

    The system gets built and connected to your real tools. No long agency timeline, no new platform for your team to learn. It runs where your work already lives.

  4. Refine

    It runs against real traffic, gets watched, and gets tightened. Edge cases surface in week one, and that is the point of staying close after launch.
