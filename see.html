The moment it arrives

An inquiry lands, and you already know what it is

When someone fills in your form, your website sends you a notification email. That copy comes to me as well, and about a minute later you get this.

  • One line saying what they want, so you do not have to open anything to know.
  • How soon they said they need it, taken only from their own words.
  • Their number and their email, and a reply already written in your voice.
  • Call back and Email open your own phone and your own mail app. I never contact your customer.

The written reply is a draft for you to send, change or ignore. It never names a price, never promises a time, and is thrown away by a checker if it tries to.

The end of the day

Whoever is still waiting, once, at the end of the day

At the end of the working day you get one email with everybody who has not heard back yet, longest wait first. If nobody is waiting, no email goes out. A quiet day stays quiet.

  • It keeps reminding you about the same person until you have dealt with them.
  • Robocalls and sales pitches are set aside, so they never nag you and never count against you.
  • An inquiry that nobody answers in a week is recorded as never answered. That number is the one thing this service is paid to bring down.

Your own page

A list you can open on a ladder

Your own private link. No login, no app to install, nothing to remember. Open it any time to see who is still waiting, tap to call or text them, and tap once more when you have dealt with them.

  • The reply is there ready to copy, so texting somebody back takes one thumb.
  • Anything that looks like junk sits in its own section with a button that says it is real, in case I got it wrong.
  • Nothing leaves the list until you say you have handled it. It does not clear itself like a missed call badge does.

The start of each month

The count, including the part that costs me money

On the second of each month you get one email: how many inquiries came in, how many you answered, and how many never got an answer at all. Junk is counted separately so it can never make the number look better than it is.

If a month comes back with nothing lost, the report says so in those words, and it says that if it keeps happening you should stop paying me. I would rather you kept the money than paid me for a problem you have already solved.

Switching it on

One forwarding rule, about three minutes

You set it, not me

In your own email settings you add one rule that sends a copy of your website form notifications to a private address I give you. I walk you through it with screenshots.

No password, ever

I never ask for your password and I never get into your inbox. A forwarding rule is one way, and it only carries what it matches.

Voicemail too, if your carrier does it

If your phone company can email you a voicemail, point those at the same address and they get logged and chased like anything else.

Stopping takes less time than starting

Delete the rule and nothing else reaches me, that minute. Nothing charges itself, so there is nothing to cancel.

The honest part

What it catches, and what it does not

It catches

  • Website contact form notifications, whatever built your site.
  • Emails people send straight to the address you point at me.
  • Voicemails, when your carrier can email them to you.

It does not catch

  • Phone calls where nobody leaves a message. There is no record of those anywhere.
  • Facebook or Instagram messages, and Google Business messages, which Google shut down in 2024.
  • Texts to your cell phone.

If most of your work comes in by phone and Facebook, tell me and I will say so plainly rather than sell you something that cannot see it.

Your customers' details

Who sees what

  • Names, numbers and messages sit in one database that only I can open. No client ever gets a login.
  • Your page is reachable only through your own private link, and it is never indexed by search engines.
  • Nothing is sold, shared or used for anything else. I never write to your customers.
  • Old closed inquiries are deleted after about 400 days. Ask me to delete everything and I will do it that day and confirm when it is done.
