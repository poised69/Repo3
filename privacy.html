What I store, and what I do not

I am one person, and this is written the way I would explain it to you on the phone rather than the way a lawyer would write it. If anything here is not clear enough, email me and I will say it better.

Last changed 12 September 2026

If you fill in the form on this site

I get your name, your email address and whatever you typed in the message box. That is all the form asks for and all it sends. It reaches me as an email and it is kept in my own records so I can answer you and remember the conversation later.

I use it to reply to you and nothing else. I do not sell it, I do not rent it, I do not add you to a mailing list, and I do not pass it to anybody who is not helping me run the service. If you would rather I deleted it, say so in a reply and it is gone the same day.

If I emailed you first

I found your business address where it was published: your own website, or your own public business page. I keep the address, your business name and what I sent you, so that I do not write to you twice about the same thing and so that I can prove what was sent.

Every email I send carries my postal address and a line telling you how to stop them. Reply with the word STOP, or say no in any words you like, and you go on a do-not-contact list the same day. That list exists so nothing reaches you again, which is why I keep your address on it rather than deleting it: an address I have forgotten is an address I could email again by accident.

If you become a client, what happens to your customers' details

You add one forwarding rule in your own email so that the notifications your website already sends you are also sent to a private address I watch. What arrives there is whatever your own form already collects, which is usually a name, an email address, a phone number, what they want, and when they sent it.

  • I never contact your customers. Not once, not for any reason. Everything that goes out to them goes out from you, from your own phone or your own email, in your name.
  • The reply I draft for you is a draft. It sits there until you send it.
  • Your list of unanswered inquiries lives on a private web page with a long unguessable address. It is marked so that search engines will not list it, and it passes no referrer when you tap a link on it. Anyone you give the address to can open it, so do not post it anywhere.
  • Nothing about your customers is sold, shared, advertised against, or shown to another client.

Where it all actually sits

The mail moves through Gmail. The automation that reads it runs on Make, in their European region. The records live in Airtable. Those three companies can technically see what passes through them, the same as they can for any business that uses them, and each has its own privacy terms. Nobody else has access. I do not run analytics on this site and there is no advertising or tracking code on it.

I am in South Korea, so that is where I work from and where the accounts are held.

How long I keep things, and how to get rid of them

While you are a client I keep your inquiry records, because the whole point of the service is that nothing is lost and that the monthly count is true. If you stop, tell me and I will delete your inquiry records and your list page; the only thing I keep is the fact that you were a client and what you paid, which I need for my own books.

If you are not a client and you just want to be forgotten, email me and say so. I will do it and tell you when it is done.
